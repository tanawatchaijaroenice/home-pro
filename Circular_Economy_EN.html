HomePro is the first retailer in Thailand to adopt the Circular Economy principle

Into its business through the Closed-Loop Circularity project, aiming to reduce environmental impact and drive sustainable growth. This initiative promotes efficient resource utilization, reduces waste, and minimizes the need for new materials by accepting returns of unwanted items from customers’ homes through the Trade-in project (แลกเก่าเพื่อโลกใหม่) while ensuring proper waste management. These recycled materials are then transformed into high-quality, eco-friendly goods known as Circular Products, further reducing environmental impact and reinforcing the company’s commitment to sustainability.

Circular Products

Made from Post-Consumer Recycled (PCR) materials, collected from HomePro customers and circularly recycled into new products.

Proper Waste Management

Old items from HomePro customers are sorted into parts and recycled into new products, which helps reduce waste and maximizes resource utilization.

Repair & Maintenance

HomePro’s professional technicians (Repair Service Center) extend product lifespan, reducing electronic waste that contributes to global warming.
