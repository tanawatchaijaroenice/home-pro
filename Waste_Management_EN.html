HOMEPRO WASTE MANAGEMENT

HomePro sustainably manage wastes as follows:

  1. Collect and separate wastes from 3 main sources from customers, associates / operations / Trade In program
  2. Hand the wastes over to our partner(s) to disassemble / clean / melt / terminate / recycle in which each process is completed accordingly to the international standards.
  3. Correct termination of unusable equipment and components while importing back usable resources into the manufacturing processes.

Refrigerant

HomePro leads the way in refrigerant collection, handling every step from removal and installation to safe disposal. Protect your home, the environment, and the planet’s future with the experts you can trust.

Insulation foam is no small matter. HomePro properly separates and safely recycles or disposes of foam, minimizing environmental impact.
