HOMEPRO WASTE MANAGEMENT

HomePro has the following sustainable waste management methods:

  1. Collect and sort waste from 3 sources: customers, employees / from operations / from the "Trade-in for a Better World" project.
  2. Forward to partners for detailed separation / crushing / washing / melting / disposal / recycling in every process and according to international standards.
  3. Bring unusable equipment and parts into the proper disposal process and bring recyclable materials back to produce new products for sale.

PROPER WASTE MANAGEMENT

Refrigerant

HomePro is a true expert in refrigerant management, handling everything from removal and installation to safe disposal, preventing CFC leaks that damage the ozone layer, for the environment and a sustainable world.

Foam and Refrigerator Insulation Management

Insulation foam is no small matter. HomePro manages foam insulation, sorts it correctly, sends it for recycling, and disposes of it safely, reducing the impact on the environment.

Hazardous Waste Management from CRT TVs

Manage CRT tubes safely and up to standard, not contaminating general waste, for a clean environment and the good health of everyone.

Tile and Sanitary Ware Management

Old tile and sanitary ware scraps are not wasted. HomePro sorts and sends them for proper recycling, turning them into eco-friendly products, reducing waste and the use of new materials.

Recycling Waste from Customer's Home

You can be confident with HomePro. We offer a packaging take-back service after installation, bringing it into the recycling process to reduce waste and add value.
